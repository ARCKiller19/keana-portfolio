## Visual Direction

Use the selected dark botanical editorial reference.

Preserve:
- near-black background
- cream/off-white typography
- forest and moss green accents
- elegant serif display typography
- clean sans-serif utility typography
- thin rules
- technical metadata
- layered botanical imagery
- subtle grids and crosshairs
- asymmetric editorial composition

Avoid:
- glassmorphism
- gradients
- generic SaaS cards
- excessive rounded corners
- pill-button overload
- fake dashboards
- random 3D objects
- generic developer portfolio layouts
